🛡 Public Safety & Emergency Center

Follow official instructions from local disaster-management authorities.

Before Landslide Warning:

  • Secure an Emergency Go-Bag with non-perishable food, water, flashlight, radio, and first aid kit.
  • Know pre-planned high ground evacuation routes and emergency shelter locations.
  • Monitor real-time alerts on LandslideGuard NER and local authority announcements.

During Active Landslide / Debris Flow:

  • Move perpendicular to the path of mudflows or falling debris towards higher ground.
  • Avoid river channels, stream beds, and steep saturated hill edges.
  • If trapped indoors, curl into a tight ball under sturdy furniture to protect your head.

After Slide Event:

  • Stay away from the landslide area. Secondary slope collapse can occur.
  • Check for injured or trapped persons near the slide perimeter without entering unstable ground.
  • Listen to battery radio broadcasts for emergency assistance instructions.
